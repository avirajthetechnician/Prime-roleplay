Beginner Guide

Get into the city today

Six steps from a fresh account to your first shift in Los Santos. Most new players are in-game within twenty minutes.

01

Create Your Account

Head to the registration page and sign up with a valid email. You'll need to verify it before the client unlocks.

02

Download the Client

Once verified, your account dashboard will show a download link for the launcher, compatible with Windows 10 and 11.

03

Connect to Prime Roleplay

Open the launcher and choose a server from the server browser based on region and population.

04

Create Your Character

Build your appearance, backstory, and starting stats. Your character's history matters for certain job applications later.

05

Choose Your Career

Pick from twelve starting careers on the jobs page, or start independent and apply for one in-city.

06

Enter the City

You'll spawn at the civilian processing center downtown. From there, the story is entirely yours to write.

Ready to Enter Prime?

Your account can be ready in under five minutes.
