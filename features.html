What's Inside

Ten systems, one city

Every system on Prime Roleplay is built to connect to the others — your job affects your money, your money affects your properties, and your reputation affects all of it.

Roleplay

Rules built to protect the story

A dedicated staff team, an active reporting system, and a rulebook focused on immersion over restriction — designed so good roleplay is always the easiest option.

Economy

Prices that respond to the server

Fuel, groceries, and business supplies shift with in-game supply and player activity, rather than sitting on a fixed price list forever.

Properties

Houses, apartments, garages

Buy, furnish, lock, and resell property across the map, with garages sized to your vehicle collection.

Vehicles

250+ handling-tuned vehicles

From work trucks to hypercars, every vehicle is tuned individually rather than sharing one generic handling profile.

Organizations

Crews with territory and structure

Organizations manage ranks, shared funds, and turf, with staff oversight to keep conflict fair and roleplay-driven.

Criminal System

Heists, contraband, and consequences

A tiered criminal system with real risk — botched jobs, informants, and evidence that law enforcement can actually use.

Law Enforcement

A full department structure

Patrol, detective, and command divisions with realistic procedure, backup requests, and training academies.

Medical System

EMS with real injury states

Injuries affect movement and stamina until treated, giving EMS characters actual work to do on shift.

Jobs

Twelve careers, real progression

Every job has a leveling path, from trainee to senior positions with more responsibility and higher pay.

Player-Owned Businesses

Build your own storefront

Set prices, hire staff, and manage inventory for restaurants, shops, dealerships, and more.

See It All In Motion

The best way to understand these systems is to spend an evening in the city.
