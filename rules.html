Server Rules

The rules that protect the story

Every rule below exists to keep roleplay fair, immersive, and enjoyable for everyone in the city. Search to jump straight to a topic.

General Rules

Using information your character couldn't realistically know — from Discord, streams, or another player's screen — to influence in-game decisions is not allowed.
In-the-moment staff rulings must be followed immediately. If you disagree, use the appeal process on the Support page rather than arguing in-scene.
Account sharing and undisclosed alternate accounts are not permitted without prior staff approval.

Roleplay Rules

Your character should react to danger the way a real person would. Running toward gunfire without cause breaks immersion for everyone involved.
Disconnecting to avoid the consequences of an ongoing roleplay scene, especially combat, results in an immediate review.
You cannot force an action's outcome onto another player's character without giving them a fair chance to react.

Criminal Rules

Characters who go unconscious from injury lose memory of the immediate event that caused it, and cannot seek direct revenge for it.
Player-to-player robberies have a 45-minute cooldown per victim, and hostage scenes are capped at a reasonable duration.

Law Enforcement Rules

Officers must escalate proportionally and document force used in every incident report filed after a scene.
Officers cannot manufacture a crime purely to justify an arrest or pursuit.

Chat Rules

Out-of-character conversation belongs in the OOC chat channel, not in-character text chat or voice.
Hate speech and targeted harassment result in an immediate and permanent ban, in both text and voice chat.

Community Rules

Server rules extend to Discord. Unsolicited advertising of other servers or communities is not permitted.
Streamers and clip creators must blur other players' personal information and avoid streaming sniping.
