Build Your Empire

From a counter to a name on the skyline

Apply for a starter license through the city hall, or buy an existing business from another player. From there, everything is yours to manage: pricing, staffing, hours, and expansion.

Successful business owners often reinvest into second locations or vertical supply chains — a farmer selling directly to a restaurant they also part-own, for example.
