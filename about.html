About Prime Roleplay

A city with a memory

Prime Roleplay started as a small whitelisted server and grew into a three-server network built entirely around one idea: the city should remember what you did in it.

Our Story

From a private server to a network

Prime Roleplay began as a handful of friends running a private FiveM server, frustrated with scripts that reset every restart and economies nobody actually managed. What started as a fix for that became a framework, then a full network.

Today the project is maintained by a full development and moderation team, with community input shaping most major features before they ship.

What Is Prime Roleplay?

A persistent GTA V roleplay city

Prime Roleplay is a heavily modified GTA V multiplayer experience focused on immersive, character-driven roleplay. Careers, businesses, law enforcement, and criminal organizations all run on interconnected systems rather than isolated minigames.

Every server shares the same rules and the same standard of roleplay, so your reputation travels with you across the network.

Our Community

Players first, always

Behind every job title and every business on the server is a real person building a story. Our moderation team exists to protect that experience — enforcing standards without getting in the way of good roleplay.

Community feedback drives our roadmap: most features on the server today started as suggestions in Discord.

Why Prime?

What makes the network different

Consistency

The same rules, economy, and quality bar apply across every server in the network.

Active Development

A full-time team ships updates, fixes, and new systems on a regular cycle.

Real Moderation

Staff are trained to protect roleplay quality, not just enforce a rulebook.

Our Vision

A city that outlasts any one story

We're building toward a network where every job, business, and organization feels permanent — shaped by players, not a script.
